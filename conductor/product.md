# Product Definition: webtrees.js

## Initial Concept
**webtrees.js** is a modern, JavaScript/TypeScript-based re-implementation of the leading online collaborative genealogy application, `webtrees`. It aims to provide a powerful, efficient, and user-friendly platform for managing family history, built on the solid foundation of the original PHP project.

## Vision & Mission
Our mission is to empower individual genealogists, collaborating families, and professional researchers with a modern, high-performance web application for preserving and sharing genealogical data. We leverage the power of Nest.js and Next.js to deliver a fast, responsive, and easily deployable alternative to the original PHP-based `webtrees`.

## Key Objectives
- **Feature Parity:** Replicate the core functionality and depth of the original PHP `webtrees` application.
- **Modern Performance:** Utilize modern JS/TS frameworks to provide a superior user experience with faster load times and smoother interactions.
- **Ease of Deployment:** Simplify the setup and hosting process, making it accessible to users with varying levels of technical expertise.

## Target Audience
- **Individual Genealogists:** Managing personal family history with precision and ease.
- **Collaborating Families:** Enabling multiple family members to contribute to a shared tree in real-time.
- **Researchers & Societies:** Providing professional-grade tools for complex genealogical studies and community projects.

## Core Features (Phase 1)
- **GEDCOM Compatibility:** Robust import and export of genealogical data using the industry-standard GEDCOM format, ensuring compatibility with other applications.
- **Interactive Family Trees:** Dynamic, visually rich family tree views that allow users to explore their ancestry intuitively.
- **User & Privacy Management:** Granular control over user roles, permissions, and data privacy to ensure sensitive information is protected and only accessible to authorized individuals.

## Key Requirements & Constraints
- **Full Internationalization (i18n):** Built from the ground up to support multiple languages, reflecting the global nature of genealogy.
- **Security & Privacy Focus:** Adherence to strict security standards and privacy protocols to safeguard user data and maintain trust.
- **Monorepo Architecture:** A unified codebase for both the Nest.js backend and Next.js frontend to streamline development and maintenance.
