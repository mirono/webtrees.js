# Product Guidelines: webtrees.js

## Prose Style
- **Friendly & Approachable:** Use a welcoming and accessible tone that is easy for families of all technical levels to understand. Avoid overly technical jargon where possible, and aim to make genealogical research feel inclusive and manageable.

## Visual Identity
- **Modern & Minimalist:** Prioritize a clean and contemporary look with a focus on readability and accessibility. Use a cohesive color palette and clear typography to create a professional and inviting environment for users to explore their history.

## UX Principles
- **Intuitive Workflows:** Guide the user through complex genealogical tasks with clear, step-by-step processes. Minimize the cognitive load by organizing features logically and providing predictable navigation.
- **Responsiveness & Speed:** Optimize the user interface for high performance and low latency. Ensure that interactive family trees and data-heavy views load quickly and respond smoothly to user input, providing a fluid and satisfying experience.

## Content & Messaging
- **Contextual Guidance:** Provide helpful, context-aware assistance throughout the application. Use tooltips, inline help, and guided tours to help users understand features and data entry requirements.
- **Reassuring & Secure:** Use clear and reassuring language, especially when handling sensitive genealogical data or errors. Clearly communicate how information is being protected and what steps the user can take to maintain their privacy.
- **Transparent Feedback:** Keep the user informed about the application's state and any ongoing processes. Use loading indicators, success messages, and informative error alerts to provide a sense of control and clarity.
